Europe travel, kept together

Roamwise helps you record European trips, estimate Schengen short-stay allowances, review destination requirements and keep travel evidence together.

Optional GPS records country observations locally. Supported travel messages and attachments become plans. Bookings never prove actual travel, and calculations do not guarantee lawful stay or entry.

The app is in release preparation. App Store and Google Play availability has not been approved.
