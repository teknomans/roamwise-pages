Privacy notice

Roamwise privacy notice — release candidate, 14 November 2026

Publisher: The Eagle Group Advantage, LLC. Contact: contact@tegadv.com.

Your travel history, nationality, approvals, checklists, ticket details and precise GPS observations are processed and stored on your device. Roamwise has no publisher-operated account or travel-data server in this build. It has no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK.

Location is optional. With permission, Roamwise accesses precise location to record country observations and help estimate travel allowances, including while the app is in the background or the screen is locked. Pause tracking in Automation or change location permissions in device settings. Force-closing the app or device restrictions may interrupt tracking.

Optional Gmail or Outlook access connects directly to your selected provider using read-only authorization. The provider receives normal connection information and handles your sign-in. Supported travel messages and attachments are downloaded to this device and parsed locally. Mailbox access is broader than the travel search; Roamwise searches for matching travel messages. It does not send or delete mail, mark messages read, use inbox data for advertising, or train AI models with it. Native authorization tokens use protected platform storage.

Roamwise’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Local records remain until you remove them or clear the workspace. Clear this workspace removes travel, imported tickets and GPS observations, stops tracking, cancels Roamwise reminders and disconnects native inbox credentials. Exported backups and original provider messages remain where you saved them. You can separately revoke access in your Google or Microsoft account. On a computer, locally configured inbox services and their saved files are separate from this browser workspace.

Backups contain personal travel data and are not encrypted by Roamwise. Protect exported files and your device. Native app storage benefits from platform protections; browser storage is not an encrypted vault. Support emails you choose to send are handled through the publisher’s normal email service.

There is no Roamwise server account to delete. Store purchases, if offered, are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms. No purchase flow is enabled in this candidate. Immigration calculations are planning aids, not a guarantee of entry or lawful stay.
