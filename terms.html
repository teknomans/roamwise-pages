Roamwise terms

Provider and contact

Roamwise is provided by The Eagle Group Advantage, LLC. Contact: contact@tegadv.com. The privacy notice describes the current handling of travel, location and inbox data: Privacy notice.

What Roamwise does

Roamwise helps organize European travel, estimate Schengen short-stay allowances, review destination preparation, and keep travel evidence together. Its calculations depend on the dates, travel history, nationality, permissions and other information available to the app. A visa, residence permit or extension must be issued by the competent authority; recording an approval in Roamwise does not obtain or validate it.

Roamwise is an independent planning aid. It is not a government service, a border record, a visa application service, or a guarantee of admission or lawful stay. Check current requirements with the relevant authority before relying on a travel plan.

Your travel records

Check imported details and correct missing or conflicting information. An airline reservation or itinerary is evidence of a plan, not proof that a border crossing happened. Location observations can be uncertain or interrupted by permissions, device settings, geography or force-closing the app. Confirm your actual travel history and approval conditions.

Optional connections and permissions

Inbox connections and location tracking are optional. Only connect an account you are authorized to access. The candidate uses read-only inbox authorization and parses supported travel messages and attachments on the device. Availability depends on the provider, authorization, connectivity and device behavior. Native inbox sync occurs on launch/resume and periodically while active; continuous delivery while the app is suspended is not promised.

Local data and deletion

The current native candidate has no publisher-operated travel account or travel-data server. Keep backups if you need your records. Exported backups contain personal data and are not encrypted by Roamwise. Protect the device and exported files. Clearing the workspace removes the app's local travel records and native inbox credentials; it does not delete original provider messages or backups saved elsewhere. See Deletion instructions.
